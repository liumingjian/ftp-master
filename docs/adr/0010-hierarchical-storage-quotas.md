# Hierarchical Storage Quotas attributed by File Version author

`ftp-master` stores every file in a single `Shared Tree` without per-`Organization` drives, while `Organizations` form an arbitrary-depth hierarchy where non-leaf nodes may hold both child `Organizations` and direct `Users`. To let `Administrators` cap storage per `Organization` and per `User` while guaranteeing that allocated quotas stay within their `Organization`'s ceiling, we introduce opt-in `Storage Quotas` (`Organization Quota` and `User Quota`, stored in exact bytes; `NULL` means unlimited, `0` allows zero bytes, and no global ceiling sits above root `Organizations`) applied uniformly to all `Users`, including `Administrators`.

**Usage attribution and accounting.** `Storage Usage` is attributed to the authoring `User` (`created_by`) of each retained `File Version` and rolled up live to that `User`'s current `Organization` and all its ancestors. All retained `File Versions` count—including files in the `Recycle Bin` until purged—deduplicated by immutable physical object key attributed to the earliest still-retained `File Version` so version restores incur zero extra bytes. Active uploads (`Upload Sessions` and folder upload batches) reserve their declared size at initiation, convert to committed usage upon completion (honoring the initial reservation even if a quota was lowered mid-transfer), and release upon `User` cancellation or 24-hour scheduler expiry.

**Strict nested partitioning with isolated unallocated pools.** For every capped `Organization`, the sum of explicit `Storage Quotas` on its nearest capped descendant `Organizations` and `Users` (passing transparently through uncapped intermediate `Organizations`) may not exceed its `Organization Quota`. Uncapped `Users` and uncapped child subtrees share only the unallocated remainder (`organization_quota - sum(nearest_capped_descendant_quotas)`), preventing uncapped members from consuming budget explicitly allocated to peers or sub-organizations. Every byte-increasing write checks the `User Quota` (if set), the nearest capped ancestor's unallocated pool (if the `User` is uncapped), and the actual subtree `Storage Usage` of every capped ancestor `Organization` as a hard physical ceiling.

**Downgrades and membership changes.** Lowering a `Storage Quota` below current `Storage Usage` is allowed—existing items stay readable, downloadable, movable, restorable, and deletable while byte-increasing writes are blocked—but lowering an `Organization Quota` below allocated descendant quotas is rejected. Moving a `User` or `Organization` preserves its `Storage Quota` and is rejected when the destination lacks sufficient unallocated budget (`User` moves may adjust `Organization` and `User Quota` atomically); historical usage follows the `User`'s current `Organization`. Deactivating a `User` leaves both usage and quota intact until an `Administrator` adjusts the quota.

## Considered Options

- **Bind `Top-level Directories` to `Organizations`:** Conflicts with the single `Shared Tree` architecture and cannot cleanly relate per-`User` quotas to `Organization` ceilings.
- **Attribute storage to the file's `Creator` Grant:** `Creator Grants` can be transferred or revoked, and collaborative edits by another `User` would charge the `Creator`'s quota instead of the editor's.
- **Allow quota overcommit or let uncapped `Users` consume total `Organization` headroom:** Simpler algebra, but allows uncapped members or peers to exhaust bytes explicitly guaranteed to capped `Users` or child `Organizations`.
- **Bill only latest versions, release on soft delete, or check only at upload completion:** Allows actual object storage to exceed quotas by up to 20 retained versions, 30 days of `Recycle Bin` content, or concurrent 20 GB uploads, and causes late failures after full uploads or during `Recycle Bin` restore.

## Consequences

- Byte-increasing write paths (`InitiateUpload`, `InitiateUploadBatch`, `SaveText`, `SaveOffice`) must enforce quota atomically using a consistent lock ordering across the membership lock (`725005`) and Shared Tree lock (`725010`). `OpenText` and write-mode `StartOfficeEdit` pre-check for `available <= 0` so `Users` do not lose edits; zero-byte operations (directory creation, rename, move, `RestoreFileVersion`, `Recycle Bin` restore) stay permitted even when over quota.
- Upload cancellation endpoints (`DELETE /api/uploads/{id}`, `DELETE /api/upload-batches/{id}`) and UI controls (including self-service listing of active uploads) are required so `Users` can immediately release reserved quota rather than waiting 24 hours for scheduled cleanup.
- Quota configuration writes commit `quota_change` `Audit Events` under the `account` category, and quota-exceeded refusals record failed `Audit Events` for the attempted write action.
- Non-`Administrator` `Users` inspect their own usage, reservations, `User Quota`, and constraining ancestor `Organization` aggregate state through a dedicated self-service endpoint without exposing other `Users`' usage or quotas.
